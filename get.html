Job Planner

A job board and money tracker for vehicle delivery drivers. Free, no account, everything stays on your phone.

Download the app

Installing it

  1. On the download page, tap the file that ends in .apk. If your phone asks whether to allow installs from the browser, allow it.
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  3. Google Play Protect will say "App blocked to protect your device". That's because the app isn't from the Play Store, not because anything is wrong with it. Tap More details.
  4. Now tap the grey words Install anyway. They look like a sentence, not a button, and they sit just above the big blue OK. Don't tap OK, that cancels the install.
  5. Open Job Planner. When it asks to send notifications, allow it. That's what makes the reminders work.

Don't want to install anything? Use the web version in your browser instead. It does the same job, with calendar reminders instead of alarms.

Updating later: open the ⋯ menu in the app and save a backup first, then download the newer file the same way. Never uninstall the app to get past Play Protect.
